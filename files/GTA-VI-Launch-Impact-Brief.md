# GTA VI Launch Impact — Project Brief

**The project:** Kalshi is paying a researcher up to $135,000 to measure GTA VI's real-world fallout — sick calls, food delivery, lost productivity. This project does the same analysis independently, as a personal portfolio piece for Cooper, and publishes it on LinkedIn.

**The angle:** The viral claim is that launch day (Thu Nov 19, 2026) will cost the US $1 billion in lost productivity. That number comes from one professor's back-of-the-napkin estimate, and his own inputs produce roughly $420 million, not $1 billion. This report audits the claim and replaces it with a defensible range, with every input sourced.

## The three questions

**1. How many people called in sick / took the day off?**
Triangulated, never directly counted:
- BambooHR platform data (3.4M US employees): PTO requests for Nov 19 running +13% vs the same Thursday in 2024/2025; Friday Nov 20 +11%. Their survey (1,530 workers): 18% of salaried workers planned to take launch day off, 32% of Gen Z.
- GameSquare survey (1,000 gamers who work full-time): 10% planned to skip launch day.
- Google Trends: US search volume for "call in sick" / "sick day" on Nov 19 vs the prior four Thursdays (baseline captured pre-launch).
- Day-off tracker: companies that gave the day — Logitech (all ~7,000 employees), Pocketpair (Nov 19–23), US Army four-day passes for soldiers who preordered, others as reported.

**2. Did DoorDash spike?**
DoorDash order data is private, so this is directional evidence, labeled as such:
- Google Trends "DoorDash" and "food delivery near me" on Nov 19 vs baseline Thursdays.
- App-store chart movement for DoorDash / Uber Eats across launch week.
- Social/news reports of delivery surges, if any surface.

**3. How much productivity did the US actually lose?**
A transparent model, not a measurement:
- Absences (low case: BambooHR's +13% PTO delta scaled to ~160M US employed; high case: survey take-off rates) × output per worker per day (~$280/day at $70K average annual output over 250 working days; sensitivity shown at higher output figures).
- Output: a low–high range, placed next to the $1B claim and the ~$420M its own math produces.
- Honest footnote, stated in the report: most one-day absence output is deferred, not destroyed — GTA V's 2013 launch left no measurable dent in BLS absence or GDP data. The report says what the number is and what it isn't.

**Hard anchors (real, countable):**
- Preorders: 9.69M units / ~$942M (Sensor Tower) — ahead of GTA V's first-day $800M.
- GTA VI peak Twitch viewership on launch day (public, countable) as the engagement anchor.
- TTWO price action across launch week.
- Kalshi's album-streams market forecast (93M first-week US streams) as the market's engagement expectation.

## What we cannot measure (stated up front in the report)

- Actual sick calls: BLS absence data lags by roughly a month.
- Actual DoorDash orders: private company data.
- Actual lost output: model only, by construction.
