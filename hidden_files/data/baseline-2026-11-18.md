## 4. Confirmed pre-launch facts

### Preorders — Sensor Tower estimates (third-party estimates; NOT confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two)
- **Brief anchor (Nov 10 reading): 9.69M units / ~$942M** — digital preorders on PS5 + Xbox Series X|S only, net of returns. Split: PS5 7.23M units / ~$703M; Xbox 2.46M units / ~$239M. Source: Sensor Tower GTA VI Sales Tracker, Nov 10, 2026, as reported by GamesRadar, Push Square, TalkEsport, and The Loadout (Nov 10–11, 2026). Sony and Microsoft each confirmed Nov 10 that GTA VI is the most pre-ordered game in their store's history, without giving totals. Source: PlayStation Blog / Microsoft, via TalkEsport, Nov 10, 2026.
- **Update since the brief (recorded so Friday doesn't compare against a stale anchor):**
  - Nov 16 reading: **14.94M units / ~$1.45B**. Source: Sensor Tower tracker via Insider Gaming, as reported by The Loadout, Nov 16–17, 2026.
  - Nov 17 reading: **15.88M units / ~$1.54B**. Split: PS5 11.83M / ~$1.15B; Xbox 4.04M / ~$390M (~75/25). ~87% of orders for the Ultimate Edition. Source: Sensor Tower tracker via RockstarINTEL / Forbes / GamesIndustry.biz, Nov 17, 2026.
  - All Sensor Tower figures are digital-only estimates; physical copies excluded; Take-Two has published no preorder count and on Nov 9 kept FY2027 net-bookings guidance at $8.0–$8.2B while saying preorders keep reaching "unprecedented levels." Source: Take-Two Nov 9, 2026 filing/results, via BitsFromBytes research summary, Nov 17, 2026.

### BambooHR — PTO and survey
Source: BambooHR, "Gamer's Day Off: PTO Requests Spike for Grand Theft Auto VI Release," published Oct 22, 2026 (read in full Nov 18, 2026). Platform data pull conducted Oct 19, 2026.

- **Platform data (3.4M+ active US-based employees):** PTO request rate for **Thu Nov 19, 2026 is ~13% higher** than the equivalent third Thursday of November in 2024 and 2025. **Fri Nov 20: ~11% higher**; Sat Nov 21 and Sun Nov 22: ~9% higher each. Caveat from the same report: PTO requests across 2026 generally are up ~2–4% year over year, but the launch dates rise faster than other 2026 dates. UK Nov 19 lift: +8.3%; Canada: +6.6%.
- **Survey (n=1,530 US full-time employees, fielded Oct 9–20, 2026, by Method Research / RepData):** **18% of salaried workers plan to take launch day off**; Gen Z 32%; Millennials 27%. **50% of gamers planning time off for an upcoming game release say they will call in sick** rather than book PTO in advance.

### GameSquare survey
Source: GameSquare Holdings, "The Grand Theft Auto Effect," announced Oct 20, 2026 (survey via Pollfish). n=1,000 full-time US employees who identify as gamers; margin of error ±3%.

- **10% plan to skip work on launch day**; another **30% are considering** taking time off.
- Of those considering time off, **36% say they would call in sick**.
- **42%** believe they may be less productive at work because of the game's arrival.
- Among those who put in for time in advance, average time requested: **4 days**. Source for the 4-day figure: GameSquare survey as summarized by Platinum Systems, updated Nov 18, 2026.

### Day-off tracker (as of Nov 18, 2026)
- **Logitech — "Logi Game Day":** announced Nov 10, 2026. **~7,000 employees worldwide** (full-time staff and interns) get **one extra paid day off** for gaming, usable on any day through November 2027, for any game. Source: Logitech announcement, via GameRant (Nov 16, 2026), AsiaOne (Nov 13, 2026), and Washington Post (Nov 18, 2026).
- **Pocketpair (Palworld developer, Japan):** announced Nov 12, 2026. Special holiday **Nov 19–23, 2026** for employees who intend to play GTA VI (5 calendar days = 3 working days: Nov 19, 20, 23). Source: Pocketpair notice via official Palworld X account, as reported by Asia Daily and Gamemeca, Nov 12–13, 2026.
- **US Army — 9th Brigade Engineer Battalion, Fort Stewart, Georgia:** soldiers who **reenlisted between Aug 1 and Nov 14, 2026** were offered a **four-day pass** over launch weekend (Nov 20–23 per one report). The Army confirmed the offer was legitimate; no evidence other bases ran the same offer. Source: GameRant, Nov 16, 2026; Washington Post, Nov 18, 2026.
- Also reported (secondary, for Friday's tracker update): Edison Motors (Canada) — four-day weekend beginning Nov 19 / operations halted Nov 19–20; Burger Motorsports — company-wide operational pause on Nov 19; Worldworthy — up to 3 days' paid leave from Nov 19; Raptor PR (UK, 9-person team) — one extra gaming day. Sources: Asia Daily, Nov 17, 2026; Gamemeca, Nov 13, 2026; DailyShorts, Nov 16, 2026.
